Token Radar

A register of protocols that record no token yet, each priced by the market its peers already trade in.

Protocols with no token, priced as if they had one

Every protocol here records no token. The radar prices each one from the market itself: the median market-cap to TVL of its live-token peers, adjusted for seven-day growth and for how long it has run without issuing. A model, not a promise.

The watchlist is the floor: the 40 protocols at or above $50M of TVL that record no token. Below it sit 160 earlier protocols the radar exists to find. Protocols whose token already trades are set aside as the pricing peers, never ranked.

How the price is made

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity. The multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL of live-token protocols in the same category when at least three trade there, and the global median of 128 live peers otherwise. Growth leans on the seven-day TVL change, capped before it can shout; maturity rewards protocols that have run longer with no token.
